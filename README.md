Prototype for research project, still in progress

- Collects participant data from a participant and their partner in a pregame survey

example:
Participant selects their interest as "Fashion"
Participant's partner selects their interest as "Music"

- Google Gemini generates 3 question prompts based on (a) current participant information (b) participant's partner information (c) system settings -- hardcoded as of now

Example question prompt generated by AI:

{"question" => "If your personal fashion sense were translated into a chaotic, over-the-top album cover or concert outfit, what wild scene or look would best capture your style energy?", "currentFactsUsed" => ["Fashion"], "partnerFactsUsed" => ["Music"]}

- Participant then responds to the prompt with a description that will be used to create an AI-generated image meant to facilitate conversation
